AI settings

The “Ask AI” buttons send your screenshot and question to an AI service, using your own API key. Nothing is sent until you press Ask. Your key stays on this device.

Leave the suggestion in place unless you know you want another model.

Good to know

  • Using your claude.ai Pro plan: Anthropic does not allow third-party tools to sign in with a Pro or Max subscription, and the API is billed separately. To use Claude without paying for API credits, choose Screenshot area, then paste in Claude.ai from the on-page button: it copies the screenshot and opens a new Claude chat for you to paste into.
  • Cost: Claude API use is billed per question to your Anthropic account, separate from a claude.ai subscription. Set a low monthly spend limit in the Anthropic console. Gemini has a free tier with usage limits.
  • Privacy: the screenshot (and page text if you tick that box) goes to the service you choose. Google may use free-tier Gemini data to improve its products, so avoid sensitive content there.
  • Keep your key private. Anyone with it can spend your credit.
